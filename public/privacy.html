Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2026

Chirkut allows users to send anonymous letters. We keep data collection minimal and use Firebase to provide authentication and message storage.

Information We Process

  • Account data: email and username when you sign up.
  • Content data: anonymous letters, optional signatures, timestamps, and style choices.
  • Operational data: basic logs from infrastructure providers for security and reliability.

How We Use Data

  • Authenticate users and deliver letters to recipient inboxes.
  • Maintain service integrity, abuse prevention, and platform security.
  • Improve product quality and troubleshoot issues.

Anonymous Letters

Letters are designed to be anonymous in the recipient experience. However, platform operators may review abuse reports and metadata for safety, moderation, and legal compliance.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is stored with infrastructure partners (for example Firebase/Google Cloud) as required to run the service.

Data Retention

We retain account and message data while accounts remain active, or as required for legal and security needs.

Your Choices

  • You can stop using the service at any time.
  • You can request account or data actions by contacting support.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@chirkut.app.
